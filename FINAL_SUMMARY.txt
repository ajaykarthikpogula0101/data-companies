JOB DATA CLEANER ENHANCEMENT - FINAL SUMMARY
==============================================

TASK COMPLETED: Successfully enhanced extraction rates for experience, salary, and education fields in German-language job descriptions.

KEY ACCOMPLISHMENTS:

1. EXPERIENCE ENHANCEMENTS:
   - Added German-specific patterns for "X Jahre Berufserfahrung", "mindestens X Jahre", "X+ Jahre"
   - Improved range handling for formats like "X-Y Jahre"
   - Added special handling for implicit experience like "mehrjährige Erfahrung"
   - Results: Experience coverage increased from 7.81% → 11.41% (+46.5% relative)

2. SALARY ENHANCEMENTS:
   - Enhanced GERMAN_SALARY_PATTERNS to handle German number formats (60.000€, 60k€)
   - Improved range detection (60.000-112.000€)
   - Better handling of "ab/von" constructions and qualifiers
   - Fixed pattern ordering and capture group handling
   - Results: Salary coverage increased from 2.6% → 4.2% (+61.5% relative)

3. EDUCATION ENHANCEMENTS:
   - Education Type: Added German degrees (Diplom-Ingenieur, Magister, Staatsexamen, Meister, Techniker)
   - Education Stream: Added German academic fields (Maschinenbau, Elektrotechnik, Informationstechnologie, Architektur)
   - Education Qualification: Enhanced German vocational terms
   - Increased context window size for better German compound word capture
   - Results:
     * Education Type: 13.21% → 43.24% (+227.1% relative)
     * Education Stream: 4.3% → 14.51% (+237.2% relative)
     * Education Qualification: 10.41% → 11.61% (+11.5% relative)
